Risk Levels

How the platform pages rate each path.

CriticalExposure gives credentials, keys or full control of the system or domain.
HighCommon persistence or privilege escalation point, or data that enables lateral movement.
MediumValuable evidence or reconnaissance data. Attackers may tamper with it to hide activity.
LowUseful context for investigations, rarely sensitive on its own.

Using These Pages

  • Defenders: monitor the Critical and High paths for unexpected reads and writes, and collect the log and artifact paths during incident response.
  • Investigators: work on a copy or a forensic image. Opening live files changes timestamps and can lock or corrupt them.
  • Testers: only access these locations on systems you own or are authorized to assess, and handle any recovered secrets as sensitive data.

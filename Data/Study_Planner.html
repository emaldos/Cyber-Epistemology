How It Works

  • Each field is a list of modules with an estimated number of hours. Modules fill your weekly hours in order, so a long module spans several weeks and short modules can share a week.
  • Beginner plans start with the foundation modules: computers, networking, Linux, Windows and security basics. Some experience skips them and starts with the field topics.
  • After 7 study weeks in a row you get a review and buffer week to catch up, redo hard labs or rest. An exam prep week also counts as a break.
  • Every certification gets a booking milestone when its final module starts and a full exam prep week right before the exam.
  • Certification names match the Certification Roadmap. Hours are estimates: if a week takes longer, regenerate the plan with a later start date.
  • The calendar export adds one all day event per week, Monday to Sunday, that does not block your calendar.
